# Temporary placeholder photography

Every image in this folder is a **temporary development asset**. None of them show
Serendib Gem House inventory. Replace them with the business's own photography
(or Sanity-managed images) before launch.

They are real gemstone photographs sourced from Wikimedia Commons under Creative
Commons licences. They were cropped (and, where noted, had the plain studio
background extended) to fit the layout ratios. Colours were not altered.

CC BY / CC BY-SA licences require visible attribution if these images are ever
published. They are not intended for production use.
