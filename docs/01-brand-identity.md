## 1. Brand Overview

**Brand Name:** Serendib Gem House

Serendib Gem House is a premium yet accessible gemstone business based in Sri Lanka.

The brand should communicate trust, elegance, quality and professionalism without appearing excessively luxurious, decorative or intimidating.

The visual identity should be timeless and simple, allowing the gemstones themselves to remain the centre of attention.

### Mission

To make fine gemstones accessible and affordable for everyone.

### Brand Personality

The brand should feel:

- Elegant
- Trustworthy
- Refined
- Professional
- Approachable
- Timeless
- Premium yet accessible

Avoid overly decorative, flashy or artificial luxury styling.

## 2. Logo Identity

### 2.1 Primary Logo Concept

The approved logo direction consists of:

- A simple crown symbol.
- A faceted gemstone beneath the crown.
- The word SERENDIB in prominent serif typography.
- The words GEM HOUSE below the brand name in smaller, refined sans-serif typography.

### 2.2 Logo Colours

- Crown: Deep Navy.
- Gemstone: Aqua / Cyan.
- Facet separators: White.
- SERENDIB: Deep Navy.
- GEM HOUSE: Deep Navy.

### 2.3 Logo Design Principles

The logo must:

- Remain recognisable at small sizes.
- Work on both digital and printed materials.
- Have a clean and balanced composition.
- Avoid unnecessary decorative elements.
- Avoid gradients and excessive visual effects.
- Maintain clear spacing between the symbol and typography.

Do not add decorative underlines, dashes, ornaments or unnecessary shapes.

### 2.4 Logo Variations

The brand should eventually support:

1. Primary full logo on white background.
2. White logo for dark backgrounds.
3. Standalone crown and gemstone symbol.
4. Single-colour version.
5. Transparent-background version.

The standalone symbol should be suitable for:
- Website favicon.
- Social media profile image.
- Product stickers.
- Gemstone packaging.
- Small printed applications.

Use the approved logo artwork as the source of truth. Do not recreate or redesign it unnecessarily during website implementation.

## 3. Brand Colour Palette

The primary colour palette consists of navy, aqua, white and pale blue.

### Primary Colours

| Colour | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Navy | #071D3B | Primary brand colour, headings, navigation and important text |
| Aqua | #12B9DC | Gemstone accent, interactive elements and highlights |
| White | #FFFFFF | Main background and clean surfaces |
| Pale Blue | #EAF5FA | Subtle backgrounds and secondary sections |

### Colour Usage Guidelines

**Deep Navy**

The dominant brand colour.

Use it for:
- Logo typography.
- Main headings.
- Navigation.
- Footer.
- Primary buttons where appropriate.

**Aqua**

Use as a supporting accent rather than a dominant background colour.

Use it for:
- Small highlights.
- Interactive accents.
- Selected icons.
- Subtle decorative elements.
- Gemstone-related visual details.

**White**

The primary website background.

Use it to create a clean and spacious presentation.

**Pale Blue**

Use for:
- Secondary section backgrounds.
- Subtle information panels.
- Soft visual separation.

Avoid using too many colours or introducing unrelated accent colours without approval.

## 4. Typography

The typography should combine elegant serif headings with clean sans-serif body text.

### Primary Typography

Use a refined serif typeface for:

- Main brand name.
- Large homepage headings.
- Important editorial headings.
- Selected promotional statements.

Suggested direction: Cormorant Garamond or a similar elegant serif font.

### Secondary Typography

Use a modern sans-serif typeface for:

- Navigation.
- Body text.
- Product specifications.
- Buttons.
- Forms.
- Prices.
- Administrative interfaces.

Suggested direction: Inter or a similar clean sans-serif font.

### Typography Principles

- Maintain clear visual hierarchy.
- Use generous line spacing.
- Avoid excessively thin text.
- Keep product information highly readable.
- Avoid excessive use of uppercase text.
- Maintain consistent font weights.

## 5. Website Visual Direction

The website should have a premium, minimal and editorial visual style.

The gemstones must remain the primary visual focus.

### General Design Principles

- Clean layouts.
- Generous whitespace.
- High-quality gemstone photography.
- Refined typography.
- Clear navigation.
- Consistent spacing.
- Subtle borders and shadows.
- Restrained use of animations.
- Responsive layouts across devices.

Avoid:
- Excessive gradients.
- Overly rounded cards everywhere.
- Excessive glassmorphism.
- Heavy animations.
- Flashy effects.
- Unnecessary decorative patterns.
- Generic luxury website clichés.

### Backgrounds

Use white as the primary background.

Pale blue can be introduced for secondary sections.

Deep navy can be used selectively for contrasting sections, particularly the footer and important brand elements.

---

## 6. Gemstone Photography Direction

Product photography is one of the most important aspects of the brand.

The website should prioritise authentic photographs of the actual gemstones being offered.

### Photography Guidelines

- Use clear, high-resolution photographs.
- Show the gemstone's actual appearance.
- Include multiple angles where available.
- Capture colour, cut and facets accurately.
- Use neutral and uncluttered backgrounds.
- Maintain consistent image presentation across the catalogue.

Do not use generic stock gemstone photographs as substitutes for actual product photographs.

Do not digitally alter gemstone photographs in ways that misrepresent the gemstone's real colour, quality or appearance.

Product photography should communicate authenticity and transparency.

---

## 7. UI Component Styling

### Buttons

Buttons should be clean and refined.

Primary actions should use Deep Navy with appropriate contrasting text.

Aqua can be used for subtle interactive accents.

### Product Cards

Product cards should feature:

- Large gemstone photography.
- Clear product name.
- Relevant specifications.
- Visible price.
- Availability status.
- Subtle hover interaction.

Avoid unnecessary card decorations.

### Availability Labels

Use clear visual labels for:

- AVAILABLE
- RESERVED
- SOLD OUT

Sold products must display a visible SOLD OUT overlay.

### Forms

Forms should have:

- Clean input fields.
- Clear labels.
- Consistent spacing.
- Visible validation messages.
- Accessible contrast.
- Simple submission buttons.

### Icons

Use a consistent, minimal icon style.

Avoid mixing unrelated icon styles.

## 8. Brand Applications

The visual identity should remain consistent across:

- Website.
- Instagram.
- Business cards.
- Packaging.
- Gemstone information cards.
- Stickers.
- Thank-you cards.
- Branded merchandise.

The website should establish the primary digital expression of the brand.

---

## 9. Brand Consistency Rules

1. Always use the official brand name: Serendib Gem House.
2. Maintain the approved navy and aqua colour palette.
3. Use serif and sans-serif typography consistently.
4. Prioritise gemstone photography over decorative graphics.
5. Maintain a clean and premium presentation.
6. Avoid unsupported gemstone quality or authenticity claims.
7. Do not modify the approved logo without approval.
8. Do not introduce additional brand colours without approval.
9. Keep the visual identity consistent across all digital and physical applications.
